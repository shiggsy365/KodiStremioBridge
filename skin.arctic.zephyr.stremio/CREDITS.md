# Arctic Zephyr Stremio

A fork of **Arctic: Zephyr - Reloaded** (`skin.arctic.zephyr.mod`) by beatmasterRS,
<https://github.com/beatmasterRS/skin.arctic.zephyr.mod>, forked from version 3.0.3
(commit `27b7bd7`, November 2025). Reloaded is itself based on **Arctic: Zephyr** by
jurialmunkey. Translations come from Reloaded's contributors on Kodi's Weblate.
